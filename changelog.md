## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-18

- Material controls from md3.io and a rebuilt searchable language picker.
- Separate range hints and errors for From and To.
- Keep the language field highlighted while its dropdown is open.
- Keep icon buttons circular when pressed.
- Let a second Escape press leave settings immediately after closing the language picker.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-18

First release of numspoken.

- Number-to-word practice in 50 languages and 72 regional/script variants, with live answer feedback.
- Custom ranges up to 999,999,999,999, continuous practice or fixed rounds.
- Number tables and English-written guides for every language.
- Light and dark themes, locally saved preferences and browser installation.
