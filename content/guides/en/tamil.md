## Joining forms

20 is _இருபது_, but 21 is _இருபத்தொன்று_. The tens change
when a unit follows. Compare the full word rather than appending
the standalone unit unchanged.

11 and 12 are special forms, _பதினொன்று_ and _பன்னிரண்டு_.
90 is _தொண்ணூறு_, while 900 is _தொள்ளாயிரம்_.

## Building hundreds

100 is _நூறு_. With a remainder it becomes _நூற்று_:
_நூற்று ஒன்று_ is 101. Other hundreds also change their ending
when another number follows.

## Thousands and lakh

_ஆயிரம்_ means thousand and _லட்சம்_ means 100,000.
Read 1,23,456 as one lakh, twenty-three thousand, four hundred fifty-six.

Written and spoken Tamil can join these parts differently. The chart
uses the written forms expected here. Vowel signs and consonant marks
remain part of the answer.
