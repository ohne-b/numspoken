## Belas and puluh

11 is _sebelas_. 12–19 use the unit followed by _belas_:
_dua belas_, _tiga belas_.

The tens use _puluh_. Put a remaining unit after it:
_dua puluh satu_ is 21 and _empat puluh dua_ is 42.

## One as se-

10, 100 and 1,000 are _sepuluh_, _seratus_ and _seribu_.
Other counts use separate words: _dua ratus_, _tiga ribu_.

Read the groups from largest to smallest. There is no word for “and”
between them: _dua ribu tiga ratus empat puluh lima_ is 2,345.

## Zero and a million

The chart uses _sifar_ for zero. _Kosong_ is also heard, especially
when reading digits. The practice form for one million is _sejuta_;
_satu juta_ expresses the same amount.
