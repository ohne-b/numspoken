## Separate words

Put the tens before the unit: _dvadeset jedan_, _trideset dva_.
The chart uses the short counting forms without a joining _i_.

The teens are single words ending in _-naest_: _jedanaest_,
_dvanaest_, _trinaest_.

## Building hundreds

100 is _sto_, 200 is _dvjesto_, and 300 is _tristo_. From 500 onwards,
the forms include _petsto_, _šesto_ and _sedamsto_.

## Tisuća

The thousands word changes with the number: _jedna tisuća_,
_dvije tisuće_, _pet tisuća_. After 11–14, use _tisuća_ too.

For compound counts, look at the last part: 23,000 is
_dvadeset tri tisuće_. One and two take feminine forms before _tisuća_.
