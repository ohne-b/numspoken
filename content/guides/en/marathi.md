## The first hundred

The number words below 100 need their own spellings. They share
pieces, but a single joining rule won't produce them all.
Compare _एकवीस_ (21), _एकतीस_ (31) and _एकेचाळीस_ (41).

Forms such as _एकोणीस_ and _एकोणतीस_ name one below the next ten.
There are exceptions, so the chart includes every number.

## Hundreds change form

Exactly 100 is _शंभर_. With a remainder, use _एकशे_:
_एकशे एक_, _एकशे वीस_.

200 is _दोनशे_, 300 is _तीनशे_, and the other hundreds follow
the same _-शे_ pattern.

## Thousands and lakh

_हजार_ means thousand; _लाख_ means 100,000. Read 12,34,567
as twelve lakh, thirty-four thousand, five hundred sixty-seven.
The comma groups follow those units.
