## Tens before units

21 is _iyirmi bir_, and 42 is _qırx iki_. Keep the words separate.
The teens follow the same order: _on bir_, _on iki_, _on üç_.

The tens are separate words to learn. For example, 40 is _qırx_
and 80 is _səksən_.

## No bir before yüz or min

100 is _yüz_, and 1,000 is _min_. For larger multiples, put the count
first: _iki yüz_, _üç min_.

Read 123,456 in descending groups: _yüz iyirmi üç min_, then
_dörd yüz əlli altı_. No word for “and” goes between the groups.

## Letters to keep distinct

_ı_ and _i_ are different letters, as are _ə_ and _e_.
The chart uses the Latin alphabet used in Azerbaijan.
