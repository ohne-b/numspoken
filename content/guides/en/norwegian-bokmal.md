## Tens before units

The chart uses the modern order: _tjueen_ is 21 and _førtito_ is 42.
Join the tens and units into one word.

You may also hear the older units-first order, such as _enogtyve_.
This practice uses the modern forms.

## Og after hundreds

Put _og_ before the final tens or units: _ett hundre og fem_ for 105,
_to hundre og førtito_ for 242.

Use _ett_ with _hundre_ and _tusen_. The standalone number in the chart
is _en_. These are gender forms of the same number.

## Thousands

Read the thousands group first, then the rest. For a remainder below
100, join it directly with _og_: _ett tusen og fem_ is 1,005.
1,000,000 is _en million_.
