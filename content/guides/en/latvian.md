## Tens and units

Put the ten first, then the unit: _divdesmit viens_, _trīsdesmit divi_.
The teens end in _-padsmit_: _vienpadsmit_, _divpadsmit_, _trīspadsmit_.

The chart uses masculine counting forms such as _viens_ and _divi_.
With a feminine noun, these may become _viena_ and _divas_.

## Building hundreds

100 on its own is _simts_. Before a smaller remainder, the chart uses
_simtu_: _simtu viens_ is 101.

The hundreds may be written as separate count-and-place words,
such as _divi simti_, or as compounds such as _divsimt_. Practice
uses the separate forms shown above.

## Thousands

1,000 is _tūkstotis_. The form changes with the count:
_divi tūkstoši_, _pieci tūkstoši_. Read the remaining hundreds,
tens and units after the thousands group.

Keep the long vowels and letters such as _š_ and _ņ_ when learning the spelling.
