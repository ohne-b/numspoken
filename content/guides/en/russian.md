## Words stay separate

21 is _двадцать один_. Add hundreds in front without a joining word:
_сто двадцать один_ is 121.

The unusual tens are _сорок_ (40) and _девяносто_ (90). Keep the soft
sign in _пять_, _шесть_ and their compounds.

## Thousands are feminine

Use _одна тысяча_ and _две тысячи_, even though standalone one and two
are _один_ and _два_.

The last digits choose the ending: _тысяча_ after 1, _тысячи_ after
2–4, and _тысяч_ after the others. 11–14 always take _тысяч_.
So 21,000 is _двадцать одна тысяча_; 12,000 is _двенадцать тысяч_.

## Stress marks

Dictionaries often mark stress with an acute accent. Ordinary Russian
writing, and this chart, leave those marks out. The letters _ь_ and _й_
are still required letters.
