## Sha and da

11–19 use _sha_: _sha ɗaya_, _sha biyu_, _sha uku_.
From 20 onwards, connect the tens and units with _da_:
_ashirin da ɗaya_ is 21.

The chart uses the common tens _talatin_, _arba'in_, _hamsin_,
_sittin_, _saba'in_, _tamanin_ and _tis'in_.

## The count follows the place word

200 is _ɗari biyu_: hundred, two. 2,000 is _dubu biyu_.
Put a remaining group after _da_: _ɗari biyu da biyar_ is 205.

This order matters. Don't move _biyu_ in front of _ɗari_ just because
English says “two hundred”.

## Spelling

_ɗ_ is a separate letter from _d_. Keep it in _ɗaya_, _huɗu_ and
_ɗari_. Ordinary Hausa writing usually omits tone and vowel-length marks;
this chart does too.
