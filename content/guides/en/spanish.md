## Joining the parts

Write 16–19 and 21–29 as one word. The accents in _dieciséis_,
_veintidós_, _veintitrés_ and _veintiséis_ belong to the spelling.

From 30 onwards, join tens and units with _y_: _cuarenta y dos_.
Don’t add _y_ between hundreds and tens: 142 is _ciento cuarenta y dos_.

## Cien or ciento

Use _cien_ for exactly 100. Use _ciento_ when another number follows:
_ciento uno_, _ciento veinte_. Before _mil_, keep _cien_: _cien mil_.

The three hundreds to watch are _quinientos_, _setecientos_ and _novecientos_.

## Uno before a noun

On its own, 21 is _veintiuno_. Before a masculine noun, it becomes
_veintiún_: 21,000 is _veintiún mil_. The same change appears in
_treinta y un mil_.

Spain, Mexico and the United States use the same number spellings here.
