## Which Korean numbers

Korean has two number systems. This practice uses Sino-Korean
_일_, _이_, _삼_, written in Hangul. Native Korean forms such as
hana and dul belong to the other system.

## Building a number

21 is _이십일_: two tens and one. Use _십_, _백_ and _천_
without an initial _일_ for 10, 100 and 1,000.

Empty places add no word. 105 is _백오_ and 1,005 is _천오_.
Zero on its own is _영_ here; _공_ is common when reading phone digits.

## Groups of ten thousand

_만_ means 10,000. 1,234,567 is 123 ten-thousands plus 4,567:
_백이십삼만 사천오백육십칠_.

Keep the spelling even when pronunciation changes across syllables.
The final six in 16 is still written _육_, as in _십육_.
