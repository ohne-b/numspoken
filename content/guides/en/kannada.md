## The joining forms

20 is _ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತು_, but 21 is _ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತೊಂದು_. The tens and units
can join and change sounds, so don't simply put two dictionary
words next to each other.

The teens have their own forms: _ಹನ್ನೊಂದು_ (11), _ಹನ್ನೆರಡು_ (12),
_ಹದಿಮೂರು_ (13). Compare the chart's spellings before extending a pattern.

## Hundreds and larger groups

_ನೂರು_ means hundred, _ಸಾವಿರ_ means thousand, and _ಲಕ್ಷ_ means
100,000. The chart keeps the separate count-and-place forms used in practice.
Joined forms also occur in ordinary Kannada.

Read 1,23,456 in three groups: one lakh, twenty-three thousand,
four hundred fifty-six. The final three digits form one group; the
groups before it have two digits each.

Vowel signs and joined consonants are part of the answer, even with
forgiving spelling selected.
