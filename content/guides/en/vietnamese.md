## Mười and mươi

10 is _mười_. From 20 onwards, use _mươi_ after the count:
_hai mươi_, _ba mươi_.

One after these tens becomes _mốt_: _hai mươi mốt_ is 21.
But 11 keeps _một_: _mười một_.

Five after a ten becomes _lăm_: _mười lăm_, _hai mươi lăm_.
The standalone number is _năm_.

## Missing tens

When a hundred has units but no tens, this chart uses _lẻ_:
_một trăm lẻ năm_ is 105. _Linh_ is another regional form.

## Thousands

The chart uses _nghìn_ for thousand; _ngàn_ is also common.
Read the thousands group first, then the remainder. Use the chart's
forms for practice here.

Tone marks change words. Keep the distinction between _mười_, _mươi_,
_một_ and _mốt_ while learning their spellings.
