Practice stops at 999,999. The million is here for reference.

## Whole words below 100

1 is _ਇੱਕ_, 21 is _ਇੱਕੀ_, and 31 is _ਇਕੱਤੀ_. Related sounds
recur, but the numbers below 100 cannot all be built by joining
a ten and a unit.

The full chart includes every form. Watch the addak mark in
_ਇੱਕ_ and _ਇਕੱਤੀ_: it marks a doubled consonant.

## Larger groups

_ਸੌ_ is hundred, _ਹਜ਼ਾਰ_ is thousand, and _ਲੱਖ_ is 100,000.
Put the count before the place word, then add any remainder.

1,23,456 reads as one lakh, twenty-three thousand, four hundred fifty-six.
The last three digits are one group; earlier comma groups have two digits.

## Script

This setting uses Gurmukhi, as used in India. Punjabi is also written
in Shahmukhi, but that script is not an answer variant in this app.
