## Masculine counting forms

This guide uses masculine units: _אחד_, _שניים_, _שלשה_.
The spelling is unpointed; vowel marks are not shown.

Biblical Hebrew also has feminine forms. The gender of the counted noun
affects the numeral, so the table is a counting reference, not a rule for
every phrase in a biblical text.

## Building hundreds

_מאה_ is feminine. Even with masculine units elsewhere, hundreds use
forms such as _שלש מאות_ and _ארבע מאות_. 200 has the dual form _מאתיים_.

## Thousands and word order

1,000 is _אלף_ and 2,000 is _אלפיים_. The table uses a consistent
largest-to-smallest order, with _ו_ joining parts. Biblical texts can
arrange compound numbers in other orders too.

Zero, _מיליון_ and _מיליארד_ are modern convenience labels in this practice mode.
They should not be read as examples of biblical numerical vocabulary.
