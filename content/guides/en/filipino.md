## The joining part

21 is _dalawampu't isa_. The _'t_ is a short form of _at_, meaning
“and”. It joins tens to a remaining unit: _tatlumpu't dalawa_ is 32.

11–19 use the _labing-_ family of forms. The sounds change in
_labindalawa_ and _labintatlo_.

## Hundreds and thousands

Compare _isa_ with _isang daan_, and _dalawa_ with _dalawang libo_.
The linker is _-ng_ after a vowel and _na_ after most consonants:
_apat na daan_.

Before a remainder, _daan_ becomes _daang_: _isang daang isa_ is 101.
An exact hundred stays _isang daan_.

## Which number system

Spanish-derived numbers are common in prices and time. This practice
uses the Filipino forms in the chart. Its word for zero is the
English loan _zero_.
