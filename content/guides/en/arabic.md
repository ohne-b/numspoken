## Units before tens

21 is _واحد وعشرون_: one and twenty. The joining letter _و_ attaches
to the following word. Continue in that order up to 99.

11 and 12 are special forms, _أحد عشر_ and _اثنا عشر_. From 13 to 19,
the unit comes before _عشر_.

## Hundreds and thousands

100 is _مئة_. 200 and 2,000 have dual forms, _مئتان_ and _ألفان_.
3,000–10,000 use the plural _آلاف_: _ثلاثة آلاف_, _عشرة آلاف_.

Join a remainder with _و_: 101 is _مئة وواحد_.

## Which grammatical form

The chart gives standalone masculine counting forms in Standard Arabic.
Numbers used with nouns can change gender and case. A feminine noun or
a different grammatical position may need another form.

Short vowel marks are omitted here. Letters such as hamza still belong
to the word, as in _أربعة_.
