## Tens and hundreds

Write tens and units separately: _двадесет један_. The hundreds
include _сто_, _двеста_, _триста_, _четиристо_ and _петсто_.

No joining word is needed in the counting forms shown here.

## Thousands

Use _једна хиљада_ and _две хиљаде_. The feminine forms of one and two
agree with the word for thousand.

The ending depends on the last digits: _хиљада_ after 1, _хиљаде_
after 2–4, and _хиљада_ after 5–9 or 0. 11–14 also use _хиљада_.

## Scripts

Serbian uses Cyrillic and Latin alphabets. Choose the script in settings;
the numbers and word order are the same. These pairs show the letters
that differ most visibly.
