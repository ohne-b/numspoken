## Below 100

The number words below 100 are individual forms. Related sounds recur,
but joining the words for a ten and a unit will usually be wrong.
Compare _ایک_, _گیارہ_, _اکیس_ and _اکتیس_.

19 is _انیس_, 29 is _انتیس_, and 39 is _انتالیس_. This
“one below” pattern helps with some groups, but not every ending in nine.

## Hundreds, thousands and lakh

_سو_ means hundred, _ہزار_ means thousand and _لاکھ_ means
100,000. The count comes before the place word.

Read 12,34,567 as twelve lakh, thirty-four thousand, five hundred sixty-seven.
The final three digits form one group, followed by groups of two.

## Script

Type in Urdu script. Letter forms and joining behavior come from
the text itself; type the logical word order and let the field display
it right to left. Short vowel marks are usually omitted.
