## Tens and units

Join the ten and unit with _și_: _douăzeci și unu_ is 21.
The teens end in _-sprezece_, with shortened forms such as
_paisprezece_ (14) and _șaisprezece_ (16).

12 has gender forms: _doisprezece_ and _douăsprezece_.
The chart shows _douăsprezece_, the form used in this practice.

## Hundreds and thousands

100 is _o sută_, 200 is _două sute_. A thousand is _o mie_;
several thousand use _mii_: _două mii_, _zece mii_.

Before _mii_, counts whose final two digits are 20–99 take _de_:
_douăzeci de mii_, _douăzeci și trei de mii_. Exact hundreds do too:
_o sută de mii_.

The hundreds and thousands are feminine, so use forms such as
_două_ with them even when the standalone number is _doi_.
