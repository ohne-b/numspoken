## Joining tens and units

11 is _kumi na moja_: ten and one. Continue with _kumi na mbili_
through _kumi na tisa_. Other tens use _na_ too:
_ishirini na tatu_ is 23.

Learn the tens as whole words, especially _thelathini_ (30),
_arobaini_ (40) and _sabini_ (70).

## Count after the place word

100 is _mia moja_ and 200 is _mia mbili_. For thousands, use
_elfu moja_, _elfu mbili_. The place-value word comes first.

Read 1,234,567 as a million, a count of thousands, then hundreds,
tens and units. Keep each part as a separate word.

## With nouns

The chart shows bare counting forms. Some numerals take noun-class
prefixes when they count people or things. For example, bare _tatu_
becomes _watatu_ with a plural noun for people.
