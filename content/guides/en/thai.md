## Two changes to remember

The word for two is _สอง_, but twenty is _ยี่สิบ_.
21 is _ยี่สิบเอ็ด_.

One on its own is _หนึ่ง_. In the final place of compound numbers,
use _เอ็ด_: _สิบเอ็ด_ (11), _สามสิบเอ็ด_ (31), _หนึ่งร้อยเอ็ด_ (101).

## Place values

_สิบ_ is ten, _ร้อย_ is hundred, _พัน_ is thousand,
_หมื่น_ is ten thousand, and _แสน_ is a hundred thousand.
Put the count before each place word.

Read 1,234,567 one place at a time: one million, two hundred-thousands,
three ten-thousands, four thousands, five hundreds, six tens and seven.
Write the words together without spaces.

## Empty places

Skip any zero place. 105 is _หนึ่งร้อยห้า_. Zero by itself is
_ศูนย์_. Keep tone marks and vowel signs when typing the answer.
