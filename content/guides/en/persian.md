## Join parts with و

21 is _بیست و یک_. Read the larger part first and connect the
next part with _و_, pronounced “o” in ordinary counting.

The same connector joins hundreds and remainders:
_صد و بیست و یک_ is 121. Keep spaces around _و_.

## Teens and hundreds

11–19 have their own forms, such as _یازده_, _دوازده_ and
_سیزده_. The hundreds include shortened forms too:
_دویست_ is 200, _سیصد_ is 300 and _پانصد_ is 500.

## Thousands

Put the count before _هزار_: _دو هزار_, _ده هزار_.
Then add the remainder with _و_. An exact thousand is _هزار_.

Use Persian _ی_ and _ک_ in the answer. The similar Arabic
letters _ي_ and _ك_ are different characters.
