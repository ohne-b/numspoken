## Tizen and huszon

10 is _tíz_, but 11–19 start with _tizen-_: _tizenegy_, _tizenkettő_.
20 is _húsz_, but 21–29 start with _huszon-_: _huszonegy_, _huszonkettő_.
The vowel is shorter inside both stems.

From 30 onwards, attach the unit directly: _harminchárom_, _negyvennégy_.

## Kettő and két

2 on its own is _kettő_. Before a place-value word it shortens to
_két_: _kétszáz_, _kétezer_. At the end of 22 it stays _kettő_.

## Writing long numbers

Write numbers up to 2,000 as one word. Above that, separate the
thousands group from a nonzero remainder with a hyphen:
_kétezer-egy_, _tizenkétezer-háromszáz_.
Exact multiples keep their joined form: _tízezer_.
