## Tens and units

Use separate words: _dvacet jedna_, _třicet dva_. The chart uses
_jedna_ for a standalone 1. _Jeden_ and _jedno_ occur with nouns
of different genders.

Keep the accents in _pět_, _devět_ and _třicet_. The long vowel in
_dvanáct_ is part of the word.

## Sto, sta, set

The hundreds change form: _sto_, _dvě stě_, _tři sta_, _čtyři sta_,
then _pět set_ through _devět set_.

Add the remainder as separate words: _dvě stě třicet jedna_ is 231.

## Thousands

1,000 is _tisíc_. Use _tisíce_ with two, three and four, and _tisíc_
with five through nineteen. Compound counts also affect the ending;
the table shows the forms used here.
