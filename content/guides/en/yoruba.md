## Adding and subtracting

Yoruba's traditional numbers use groups of twenty. 20 is _ogún_,
40 is _ogójì_, and 60 is _ọgọ́ta_.

Some numbers add to a lower group, while others subtract from the
next one. 15 is five short of twenty. The word pieces often contract,
so the full chart gives the resulting spellings.

## Larger groups

Hundreds and thousands have their own forms, including _ọgọ́rùn_
(100), _igba_ (200) and _ẹgbẹ̀rún_ (1,000). They are not all
ordinary multiples of a single word for hundred.

The million entry uses the modern loan _mílíọ̀nù_.

## Tone and vowel marks

_ẹ_ and _ọ_ are distinct vowels. Acute and grave accents mark tone;
an unmarked vowel has mid tone. Keep both kinds of marks when learning
the words. Other counting traditions and spelling conventions exist;
these exercises use the forms in this chart.
