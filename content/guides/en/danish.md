## Reading backwards within the tens

21 is _enogtyve_: one-and-twenty. Use that order through 99:
_syvogfyrre_ is 47.

1 can be _en_ or _et_, depending on the noun. The chart uses _et_
on its own and _en_ inside forms such as _enogtyve_.

## Fifty, sixty, seventy

The words for 50–90 come from counting in twenties. Learn the short
forms as a set: _halvtreds_, _tres_, _halvfjerds_, _firs_, _halvfems_.
The “half” forms mean half a twenty below the next whole group.

## Building hundreds

Add _og_ before a remainder under 100: 142 is
_et hundrede og toogfyrre_. The units still come before the tens.

Thousands use _tusind_ or _tusinde_. A million is _en million_;
several million take _millioner_.
