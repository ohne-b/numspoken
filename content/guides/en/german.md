## Units come first

For 21–99, put the unit before the ten and join them with _und_:
_dreiundvierzig_ is 43. Write the whole number as one word.

_Eins_ loses its final “s” before _und_: _einundzwanzig_. It keeps the “s”
at the end of 101: _einhunderteins_.

## Shortened spellings

16 and 60 use _sech-_; 17 and 70 use _sieb-_. Compare _sechzehn_ with
_sechsundzwanzig_, and _siebzig_ with _siebenundzwanzig_.

30 is _dreißig_, with _ß_ rather than _z_.

## Hundreds and thousands

Join the hundreds, thousands and remainder without spaces:
_zweitausendvierhundertsechsundfünfzig_ is 2,456.

The chart keeps the initial _ein_ in _einhundert_ and _eintausend_.
_Million_ is a separate, capitalized noun: _eine Million_.
