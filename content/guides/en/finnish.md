## Teens and tens

For 11–19, add _toista_ to the unit: _yksitoista_, _kaksitoista_.
For the tens, use _kymmentä_: _kaksikymmentä_ is 20.

The unit follows directly: _kaksikymmentäkolme_ is 23. Write the
whole number together.

## Changing the place-value word

100 is _sata_, but 200 is _kaksisataa_. Likewise, 1,000 is _tuhat_
and 2,000 is _kaksituhatta_.

These endings matter: the plural groups use _sataa_ and _tuhatta_,
not the standalone words _sata_ and _tuhat_.

## Reading a long number

In 123,456, read 123 first, attach _tuhatta_, then add 456.
The result is one long word. There is no word for “and” between groups.
