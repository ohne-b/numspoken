## Counting in twenties

20 is _ოცი_, 40 is _ორმოცი_, 60 is _სამოცი_ and 80 is _ოთხმოცი_.
Between those points, attach the remainder with _და_.

31 is twenty plus eleven: _ოცდათერთმეტი_. 75 is sixty plus fifteen:
_სამოცდათხუთმეტი_. The whole form is one word.

## The final ი

100 is _ასი_. Before a remainder, it becomes _ას_: _ას ერთი_ is 101.
The same change appears in other hundreds, such as _ორასი_ and _ორას ერთი_.

## Thousands

1,000 is _ათასი_, but 1,001 is _ათას ერთი_. The word before the
thousands keeps its own ending: _ორი ათასი_, _სამი ათასი_.

Read a larger number by separating its thousands group from its remainder.
The base-20 pattern still applies inside each group.
