## Reading the characters

This practice uses kanji: _一_, _二_, _三_. Type the written number
in kanji rather than its reading in kana or Latin letters.

21 is _二十一_: two tens and one. Use _十_, _百_ and _千_
without an initial _一_ for 10, 100 and 1,000.

## Skip empty places

105 is _百五_, and 1,005 is _千五_. There is no zero character
between the nonzero parts. Zero by itself is _零_.

## Ten-thousand groups

_万_ means 10,000. Read 1,234,567 as 123 ten-thousands plus 4,567:
_百二十三万四千五百六十七_.

## Spoken forms

Some readings change: 300 is sanbyaku, 600 is roppyaku and 800 is
happyaku. Their written forms still follow the chart: _三百_,
_六百_, _八百_. Counters for people, objects or days can introduce
other readings; these exercises use bare numbers.
