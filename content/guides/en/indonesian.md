## Belas and puluh

11 is _sebelas_. For 12–19, put the unit before _belas_:
_dua belas_, _tiga belas_.

The tens use _puluh_: _dua puluh_ is 20. A remaining unit follows:
_dua puluh satu_ is 21. Keep spaces between the words.

## Se- means one

10, 100 and 1,000 use the prefix _se-_:
_sepuluh_, _seratus_, _seribu_.

Larger multiples use the ordinary count: _dua ratus_, _tiga ribu_.
Then read the remainder from largest to smallest, without a word for “and”.

## A larger example

1,234,567 is _satu juta dua ratus tiga puluh empat ribu
lima ratus enam puluh tujuh_.
The word _ribu_ separates the thousands group from the final three digits.
1,000,000 is _satu juta_.
