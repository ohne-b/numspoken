## Largest part first

11 is _on bir_, and 21 is _yirmi bir_. The tens come before the units,
with a space between words. There is no joining word.

Each ten has its own name: _yirmi_, _otuz_, _kırk_, _elli_,
_altmış_, _yetmiş_, _seksen_, _doksan_.

## Yüz and bin

Use _yüz_ for 100 and _bin_ for 1,000, without an initial _bir_.
For several hundred or thousand, put the count first:
_iki yüz_, _üç bin_.

1,234,567 is _bir milyon iki yüz otuz dört bin beş yüz altmış yedi_.
The word _bin_ separates the thousands group from the remainder.

## Dotted and dotless i

_ı_ and _i_ are different letters. Keep them distinct in
_sıfır_, _kırk_, _altmış_ and _iki_. One million is _bir milyon_.
