## Dropping a vowel

Before _uno_ and _otto_, the tens lose their last vowel:
_ventuno_, _ventotto_, _trentuno_, _trentotto_.

The number stays one word. 42 is _quarantadue_, and 342 is
_trecentoquarantadue_.

## Tre at the end

On its own, 3 is _tre_. At the end of a compound it takes an accent:
_ventitré_, _trentatré_, _centotré_.

## Cento and mille

_Cento_ drops its final “o” before _ottanta_: _centottanta_ is 180.
200 is _duecento_, 300 is _trecento_, and the pattern continues to 900.

1,000 is _mille_. For several thousand, use _mila_: _duemila_,
_ventimila_. A million is written separately: _un milione_.
