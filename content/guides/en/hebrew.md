## Counting without a noun

Use the feminine forms for counting: _אחת_, _שתיים_, _שלוש_.
The chart follows the unpointed spellings used in practice. Shorter
spellings such as _שתים_ and _שלש_ also occur.

Tens come before units, joined with _ו_: _עשרים ואחת_ is 21.
Attach the _ו_ to the next word.

## Hundreds and thousands

200 is _מאתיים_, and 2,000 is _אלפיים_. The other hundreds use
the feminine word _מאות_: _שלוש מאות_, _ארבע מאות_.

3,000–10,000 use special connecting forms: _שלושת אלפים_,
_ארבעת אלפים_, _עשרת אלפים_. Above that, the thousands group
uses masculine numerals, such as _אחד עשר אלף_ for 11,000.

## Gender in sentences

A counted noun can require a masculine number instead. These exercises
show a bare number, so the chart's counting form is the one to use.
