## Tens followed by units

11–19 start with _አስራ_: 12 is _አስራ ሁለት_. From 20 onwards,
say the ten and then the unit: _ሃያ አንድ_ is 21.

The tens have their own words. 30, _ሰላሳ_, is not formed by simply
joining three and ten.

## Hundreds and thousands

Put the count before _መቶ_ (hundred) or _ሺ_ (thousand):
_ሁለት መቶ_, _ሁለት ሺ_. The smaller part follows with a space.

The app displays 0–9 digits even though Ethiopic numeral symbols also exist.
Type the number's words, not those symbols.

## Latin script

The Latin-script setting transliterates the same number words. Its spelling
follows the converter's convention; other dictionaries may romanize them differently.
