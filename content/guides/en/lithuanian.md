## Counting forms

The chart uses masculine forms: _vienas_, _du_, _trys_. Numbers
agree with nouns in Lithuanian, so forms inside a sentence can differ.

21 is _dvidešimt vienas_. Tens and units stay separate. The teens
are single words, including _vienuolika_, _dvylika_ and _trylika_.

## Building hundreds

100 is _vienas šimtas_. For several hundred, use _šimtai_:
_du šimtai_, _trys šimtai_. Add the remainder with spaces and no
joining word: _du šimtai trisdešimt vienas_ is 231.

## Thousands

1,000 is _vienas tūkstantis_. Counts ending in 2–9 use _tūkstančiai_,
while tens and the teens use _tūkstančių_. Compare _du tūkstančiai_
with _dešimt tūkstančių_.

For a long number, apply the same counting rules within the thousands
group, then read the remaining three digits.
