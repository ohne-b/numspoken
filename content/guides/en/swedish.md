## Tens first

Join the ten to the unit: _tjugoett_, _trettiotvå_, _fyrtiotre_.
There is no joining word between them.

13–19 end in _-ton_, but the stems change: _tretton_, _fjorton_,
_arton_. Compare those with the tens _trettio_, _fyrtio_, _åttio_.

## En and ett

Both mean one. The chart uses the neuter _ett_ for counting.
At 1,000 it leaves out the optional _ett_: _tusen_. A million is _en miljon_.

## Hundreds and thousands

Read the groups from largest to smallest: _tvåtusenfyrahundrafemtiosex_
is 2,456. Written number words can get long; the place-value words
_tusen_ and _hundra_ mark the groups.

Zero places add nothing: 105 has no word for zero between _hundra_ and _fem_.
