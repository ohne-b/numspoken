## Units before tens

21 is _eenentwintig_: one, _en_, twenty. Continue with
_tweeëntwintig_, _drieëntwintig_ and so on.

The two dots in _tweeëntwintig_ and _drieëndertig_ separate vowel sounds.
They are part of the spelling, not decoration.

## One long word

Write numbers below a thousand together: _honderddrieënveertig_ is 143.
The group before _duizend_ stays attached too. Put a space after that group
when more follows: _tweeduizend vierhonderd_.

The chart uses accented _één_ for a standalone 1. Inside compounds it is
_een_, as in _eenentwintig_. Don’t add a second _en_ after _honderd_:
101 is _honderdeen_.
