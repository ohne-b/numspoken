## Joining with e

Join tens and units with _e_: _trinta e dois_. Use it after hundreds too:
_duzentos e trinta e dois_.

Exactly 100 is _cem_. With a remainder it becomes _cento_: _cento e um_.
200–900 have their own forms; keep _quinhentos_ and _novecentos_ in mind.

## Thousands

1,000 is just _mil_. Read 123,456 as a thousands group followed by a
hundreds group: _cento e vinte e três mil quatrocentos e cinquenta e seis_.

Use _e_ after _mil_ before a remainder under 100 or an exact hundred:
_mil e dois_, _mil e duzentos_.

## European Portuguese

The chart uses Brazilian spelling. Portugal differs at 16, 17 and 19.
The rest of the joining pattern is the same.

| Number | Portuguese |
| -----: | ---------- |
|     16 | dezasseis  |
|     17 | dezassete  |
|     19 | dezanove   |
